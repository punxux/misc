# The 7 simplified Hermetic principles are: 

1) Mentalism (everything is mind), 
2) Correspondence ("As above, so below"), 
3) Vibration (nothing is still, everything moves), 
4) Polarity (everything has two sides or opposites), 
5) Rhythm (everything flows in cycles), 
6) Cause and Effect (nothing happens by chance), and 
7) Gender (everything has a masculine and feminine principle). 

These principles describe universal laws believed to govern reality and our experiences
