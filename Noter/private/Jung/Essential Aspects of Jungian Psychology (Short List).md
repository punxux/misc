
### **1. The Shadow**

The unconscious aspects of yourself you reject, deny, or avoid—often containing both darkness _and_ hidden strengths.

**Question:** _What part of myself do I avoid acknowledging, even though it keeps showing up in my reactions?_

### **2. Archetypes**

Universal patterns or primordial images (e.g., Hero, Wise Old Man, Anima/Animus, Trickster) that shape behavior, emotions, dreams, and personal myths.

**Question:** _Which archetypal role do I unconsciously fall into—and which archetype within me is asking to be recognized?_

### **3. The Persona**

The social mask you wear to fit into society—useful but limiting if you mistake it for your true identity.

**Question:** _Where in my life am I presenting a mask that no longer represents who I truly am?_

### **4. The Anima & Animus**

The unconscious feminine aspect in men (anima) and the unconscious masculine aspect in women (animus); integrating them leads to inner balance and emotional depth.

**Question:** _What inner qualities—emotional, intuitive, assertive, or rational—have I neglected because they didn’t fit my identity?_
### **5. Individuation**

The lifelong process of becoming your true, integrated self— aligning conscious life with the deeper unconscious psyche.

**Question:** _What is the next small step I can take to become more aligned with the person I sense I’m meant to be?_
### **6. The Self**

The central, unifying archetype that represents wholeness, meaning, and the totality of who you are (not just the ego).

**Question:** _When do I feel closest to the deeper center of my being, the part that doesn’t change with circumstances?_
### **7. Synchronicity**

Meaningful coincidences that reveal underlying psychological or symbolic connections, reflecting the psyche’s dialog with reality.

**Question:** _What recent “coincidence” felt meaningful, and what inner message might it be pointing toward?_
### **8. The Collective Unconscious**

A shared layer of the unconscious that carries archetypes, myths, and symbolic patterns across cultures and eras.

**Question:** _Which myths, symbols, or stories have always resonated with me—and why might they mirror something universal in my psyche?_
### **9. Active Imagination**

A technique to intentionally engage with the unconscious through symbolic dialogue, imagery, or creative expression.

**Question:** _If I allowed an inner image or dream figure to speak, what might it try to tell me right now?_
### **10. Complexes**

Emotionally charged clusters of ideas, memories, and feelings (e.g., mother complex, power complex) that influence behavior automatically.

**Question:** _Which emotional trigger has a disproportionate effect on me, and what might that reveal about an underlying complex?_
